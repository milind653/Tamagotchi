What is Tamagotchi?

A pet that lives in your pocket
(and now your browser)

Tamagotchi (たまごっち) is a virtual pet game that took Japan, and then the world, by storm in 1996. The name mixes tamago (卵, egg) and watch: a little egg-shaped toy you carry around and care for.

The idea is simple and surprisingly emotional. Your pet is born from an egg and needs you: it gets hungry, makes a mess, gets lonely and gets sleepy. Take care of it and it grows into an adult. Ignore it and it may sadly pass away.

This version brings that magic to your screen, with an account so your pet is always waiting for you.

How to play

Four steps to a happy pet

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Create a free account in seconds. Your pet is saved to your account, so you can come back on any device.

  2. 2

    Choose an egg

    Pick one of twelve pets, from a real-life Shiba or calico kitten to a squishy Mochi, give it a name, and watch it wobble until it hatches.

  3. 3

    Take care of it

    Watch the five meters: fullness, happiness, energy, cleanliness and health. Keep them topped up.

  4. 4

    Watch it grow

    Your pet changes as it ages. Good care unlocks the Angelic form. Neglect may make a Rascal.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Is it free?

      Yes. Just sign up with a username and password and start playing.

      What happens when I close the page?

      Your pet keeps living. Hunger, mess and sleepiness carry on in real time, so check in now and then. Up to 12 hours of time away is counted each visit.

      Can my pet really die?

      If it is badly neglected for a long time, yes. It will move to your memorial, and you can adopt a new egg and try again. Don't worry, there are plenty of warnings before it gets that far.

      How often should I check on it?

      A few times a day is plenty. A baby needs a bit more attention, and an adult is more relaxed.

      What changes how my pet grows up?

      Care mistakes, such as letting it starve, stay lonely or get sick from mess, decide its adult form: Angelic, Normal or Rascal.
